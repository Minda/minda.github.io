Writing

  • From S3Q Theory to Implementation: Towards an Architecture for Machine Qualia (peer-reviewed; IEEE International Conference on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, SMC 2026, Bellevue, WA, Oct 2026). Tetiana Grinberg, Katrina Schleisman, Patryk Laurent, Bogdan Udrea, Minda Myers, Brian Aufderheide, Luis El Srouji, Doyle Groves, Kevin Schmidt. Proposes a five-layer architecture for implementing the S3Q theory of consciousness, mapping its three conditions (grounded sensorimotor experience, internal world-model simulation, and coherence between prediction and observation) onto one pipeline, with a developmental bootstrap sequence and testable predictions. Preprint on arXiv.
  • Building Safe & Verifiable Neuromorphic Agents (Mind Guild, Oct 2026) — could spiking neural networks on brain-like hardware give us an interpretable, energy-efficient learner whose safety is proved rather than only tested? The essay lays out a plan to apply formal verification to small networks seeded from biological nervous systems, adding capabilities one verified range at a time, and is candid about the open problems and the risks.
